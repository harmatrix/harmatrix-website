03 — About

An independent studio in Montevideo

Harmatrix was founded in Montevideo in 2025 to do one thing well: build software that lasts, for clients who care about craft.

Led by its founding engineer — with two decades across data engineering, machine learning, and product development — Harmatrix works as a hands-on partner to startups and established companies alike, serving clients in Uruguay and exporting software services across the Americas. We keep the team deliberately small, the standards high, and the communication direct.
